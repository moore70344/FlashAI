# AI-Powered One-Person Business Framework

## The $100K Solo Operator Blueprint

This framework is a complete, executable system for building a one-person business
that generates $100K+/year using AI as your operational leverage. No employees.
No funding. No 18-month runway fantasies.

Built specifically for the FlashAI ecosystem and applicable to any AI-powered solo operation.

## Core Principles

```
You don't need employees. You need systems.
You don't need funding. You need margin.
You don't need scale. You need repeatability.
AI is your unfair advantage -- if you know what to build.
```

## Rules

1. If it needs a team, delete it
2. If it doesn't generate cash in 90 days, it's not a business
3. No "find your passion" -- find the pain that pays
4. Revenue math must work on paper before you build anything
5. AI handles execution; you handle strategy and client relationships

## How to Use This Framework

1. **Start with 01** -- Input your situation, get your model
2. **Work through 02-06** -- Build your offer, acquisition, and delivery systems
3. **Execute 07** -- Follow the 90-day plan week by week
4. **Reference 08-09** -- Use prompts daily, avoid failure modes
5. **Revisit 10** -- Monthly reality checks against projections

Every section includes fill-in-the-blank templates. Replace brackets `[like this]`
with your specifics. The framework does the thinking; you do the executing.
