# 03 - Client Acquisition Machine

## The System That Fills Your Pipeline

Marketing is not a creative exercise. It's a volume game with conversion math.
Know your numbers. Work the numbers. The numbers work.

---

## Month 1: First 3 Clients (Proof of Concept)

The only goal in Month 1: **get 3 humans to pay you money.** Not 30. Not 300. Three.
This proves the offer works and gives you case studies.

### Where to Find Them

Pick TWO channels maximum. Do not spread across five platforms.

**High-intent channels (people already looking for solutions):**

| Channel | Best for | How to use |
|---------|----------|------------|
| LinkedIn | B2B services, consulting, SaaS | Direct outreach to your ICP |
| Twitter/X | Tech, creator economy, SaaS | Value posts + DM conversations |
| Reddit | Niche communities, technical services | Helpful answers + profile link |
| Facebook Groups | Local services, specific niches | Genuine participation + offers |
| Upwork/Fiverr | Any service (use as validation only) | Low-price entry to get testimonials |
| IndieHackers | SaaS, tools, developer services | Show building in public |
| Slack/Discord | Industry-specific communities | Become the helpful expert |

**Selection criteria:**
1. Where does my exact avatar spend time?
2. Where can I start conversations (not just broadcast)?
3. Where can I demonstrate expertise before asking for anything?

### What to Say: The Outreach Framework

**Cold DM/Email structure (The PAS-O Framework):**

```
SUBJECT/OPENER: [Personalized observation about their business]

PAIN: I noticed [specific problem you observed in their business].
AGITATE: Most [their role] struggle with this because [why it's hard].
SOLUTION: I help [their type] achieve [specific outcome] by [your method].
OFFER: Would it be useful if I [specific low-commitment next step]?

No pitch. No attachments. No "hop on a call."
```

**Example - Service Arbitrage (Content):**

```
Hey [Name],

I've been following your posts on [platform] -- your take on [topic] was solid.

I noticed your posting frequency dropped off in December. That's common --
most founders hit a wall creating consistent content while running a company.

I build content engines for B2B founders. I handle the writing, you handle
approvals. My clients typically see 3-5x engagement increase within 60 days.

Would it be helpful if I drafted 3 sample posts in your voice so you can see
what that looks like? No strings.

[Your name]
```

**Example - Productized Service (Conversion Audit):**

```
Hey [Name],

I just went through [their website] and spotted 3 conversion leaks that are
likely costing you [estimate based on their traffic/industry].

I run conversion audits for e-commerce brands in the [X] range and these
patterns are fixable in under a week.

Want me to send over the 3 quick wins I found? No charge for those.

[Your name]
```

### How to Close: The Sales Process

**Step 1: Free value** (the outreach above)
**Step 2: Discovery call** (15 min max -- not 60)
**Step 3: Proposal** (AI-generated, customized in 20 min)
**Step 4: Close** (same call or within 24 hours)

**Discovery call script (15 minutes):**

```
[2 min] "Tell me about [their business] -- what's working well right now?"
[3 min] "What's the biggest bottleneck with [area you serve]?"
[3 min] "What have you tried so far? What worked, what didn't?"
[2 min] "If we could [solve their stated problem], what would that be worth
         to your business over the next 12 months?"
[3 min] "Here's what I'd recommend..." [present your package]
[2 min] "Does this make sense for where you are right now?"
```

If they say yes: send invoice immediately. Not "I'll send a proposal Monday."
Same day. Same call if possible.

### Conversion Math

```
OUTREACH VOLUME:     [X] messages per day
RESPONSE RATE:       10-15% (cold), 25-40% (warm)
CALL BOOKING RATE:   30-50% of responses
CLOSE RATE:          25-40% of calls

MATH TO GET 3 CLIENTS:
- 3 clients / 0.30 close rate = 10 calls needed
- 10 calls / 0.40 booking rate = 25 responses needed
- 25 responses / 0.12 response rate = ~210 outreach messages
- 210 messages / 30 days = 7 messages per day

That's it. 7 personalized messages per day for 30 days = 3 paying clients.
```

## Month 2-3: Scale to 10 Clients

### Shift from Pure Outbound to Inbound-Assisted

**Continue outbound:** 5-10 messages/day (now with case studies from Month 1)

**Add content engine:**

| Platform | Frequency | Content type | Time investment |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| LinkedIn | 5x/week | Value posts + case studies | 30 min/day (AI drafts) |
| Newsletter | 1x/week | Deep-dive on your expertise | 1 hour/week (AI drafts) |
| Twitter/X | 3-5x/week | Insights + engagement | 20 min/day (AI drafts) |

**Content formula that generates leads:**

1. **40% Problem-aware posts:** Name the pain your audience has
2. **30% Social proof posts:** Results, testimonials, case studies
3. **20% Process posts:** Show how you work (builds trust)
4. **10% Direct offers:** Clear CTA to work with you

### Referral System

After delivering results, use this script:

```
"[Client name], glad we hit [specific result]. Quick question --
do you know 2-3 other [their role] who are dealing with [the problem
you solved]? I have bandwidth for [X] more clients this quarter
and I'd rather work with people in your network than cold prospects.

I'm happy to [incentive: discount on next month / free bonus service /
gift card] as a thank you for any introduction."
```

**Referral math:** If 50% of happy clients refer 1 person, and 50% of referrals
close, every 4 clients generates 1 new client automatically.

## Month 4+: Systematize to $100K

### Pick ONE Inbound Channel and Go Deep

| Channel | Best for | Timeline to ROI |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| SEO + Blog | Evergreen service businesses | 4-8 months |
| LinkedIn content | B2B services | 2-4 months |
| YouTube | Info products, SaaS | 4-8 months |
| Paid ads (Meta/Google) | Productized services, SaaS | 2-4 weeks |
| Podcast guesting | Authority building, B2B | 2-4 months |

### The AI Content Engine

**Weekly content production system:**

```
TIME: 2 hours/week total

Monday (30 min):
- Review industry news/trends (AI curates)
- Pick 5 content angles for the week
- AI drafts all 5 posts

Tuesday-Friday (15 min/day):
- Review and edit one post
- Publish
- Spend 10 min engaging with comments/replies

Weekend (30 min):
- AI drafts weekly newsletter from best-performing post
- Review and send
```

### Email List: The Asset That Compounds

**Lead magnet options (all AI-created):**
- Checklist: "The [X]-Point [Outcome] Checklist"
- Template: "[X] Templates for [Outcome]"
- Calculator: "Calculate Your [Metric]"
- Mini-course: "3 Days to [Quick Win]"
- Swipe file: "[X] Proven [Things] You Can Steal"

**Nurture sequence (7 emails, AI-written, you edit):**

| Email | Timing | Purpose | Subject line formula |
|-------|--------|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | Immediate | Deliver lead magnet + introduce yourself | "Here's your [lead magnet]" |
| 2 | Day 2 | Share your story + credibility | "Why I [started/focus on] [topic]" |
| 3 | Day 4 | Teach something valuable | "The [X] mistake costing you [Y]" |
| 4 | Day 7 | Case study / social proof | "How [client] achieved [result]" |
| 5 | Day 10 | Address main objection | "[Objection]? Here's the truth" |
| 6 | Day 14 | Soft pitch | "Is [problem] still keeping you up?" |
| 7 | Day 17 | Direct offer + deadline | "Last chance: [offer details]" |

### The 3 Metrics That Matter

Track these weekly. Ignore everything else.

```
1. PIPELINE: How many qualified conversations did I start this week?
   Target: [X] per week

2. CLOSE RATE: What % of proposals turned into clients?
   Target: 30%+ (if below, fix your offer or qualification)

3. CLIENT LIFETIME VALUE: How much does each client pay over time?
   Target: $[X] (if too low, add retention/upsell mechanisms)
```

Everything else -- followers, likes, impressions -- is vanity.
Pipeline, close rate, LTV. That's the business.
