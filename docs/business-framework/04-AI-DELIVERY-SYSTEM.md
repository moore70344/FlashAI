# 04 - AI-Powered Delivery System

## Map the Exact Fulfillment Workflow

The goal: deliver excellent work in 20% of the time it would take manually.
AI handles volume. You handle judgment.

## Client Onboarding

### Intake Form

AI generates a customized intake form based on your service type. The form
captures everything needed to start work without a second call.

```
STANDARD INTAKE FIELDS:

Business info:
- Company name
- Website URL
- Industry/niche
- Annual revenue range (for B2B -- helps with pricing conversations)
- Number of employees

Project-specific:
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- What have you tried before?
- Who is your target audience?
- What does success look like in 90 days?
- What's your deadline?

Assets needed:
- Brand guidelines (if any)
- Existing content/materials
- Login credentials (if applicable)
- Key contacts

Communication preferences:
- Preferred update frequency
- Best communication channel
- Decision maker(s) for approvals
```

**Tool:** Use Tally, Typeform, or Google Forms. AI writes the form questions
customized to your service.

### Kickoff Process

```
Day 0 (30 minutes):
1. Client submits intake form
2. AI processes responses into a project brief (5 min)
3. You review brief, note any gaps (10 min)
4. AI generates SOW/contract from brief template (5 min)
5. You review SOW, send to client (10 min)

Day 1 (15 minutes):
1. Client signs SOW + pays invoice
2. AI generates kickoff email with timeline + expectations (5 min)
3. You review and send (5 min)
4. Add to project management board (5 min)
```

```
STATEMENT OF WORK

Client: [Name]

Date: [Date]

[AI fills based on intake + your service packages]

1. [Deliverable with specifics]

3. [Deliverable with specifics]

- Kickoff: [Date]
- First draft/delivery: [Date]
- Revision period: [Date range]
- Final delivery: [Date]

- [X] rounds of revisions

- [Exclusion 1]
- [Exclusion 2]

REVISION POLICY:
- Revisions within scope are included up to [X] rounds
- Additional revisions billed at $[X]/hour
- Revisions requested after final approval are new projects
```

## Service Delivery Workflow

### Template: Service Arbitrage / Productized Service

**Step 1: Research & Strategy**

```
Tool: Claude / ChatGPT / Perplexity
Time: 15-30 minutes

PROMPT TEMPLATE:
"Analyze [client's business/industry]. Research:
1. Top 5 competitors and their [relevant strategies]
2. Key trends in [their industry] for [current year]
3. [Client's target audience] -- their pain points, desires, objections
4. Gaps in the market that [client] can exploit

Output: A strategic brief with specific, actionable recommendations.
Format as a professional document."

YOUR INVOLVEMENT:
- Review AI output for accuracy (10 min)
- Add your expert insights AI can't provide (10 min)
- Flag anything that needs client input (5 min)

OUTPUT: Strategic brief document
```

**Step 2: Core Deliverable Production**

```
Tool: Claude / ChatGPT (for writing), Midjourney/DALL-E (for visuals),
      Cursor/Replit (for code)
Time: 30-90 minutes depending on complexity

PROMPT TEMPLATE:
"Based on this strategic brief: [paste brief]

Create [specific deliverable]:
- Format: [specifications]
- Tone: [brand voice notes from intake]
- Length: [word count / page count / duration]
- Must include: [required elements]
- Must avoid: [things client doesn't want]
- Target audience: [from intake form]

Reference materials: [paste relevant context]"

YOUR INVOLVEMENT:
- Craft the prompt with client-specific details (10 min)
- Review first draft for quality and accuracy (15-30 min)
- Make expert-level edits AI can't (15-30 min)
- Format for client presentation (10 min)

OUTPUT: First draft of core deliverable
```

**Step 3: Quality Enhancement**

```
Tool: Claude / Grammarly / industry-specific tools
Time: 15-30 minutes

PROMPT TEMPLATE:
"Review this [deliverable type] for:
1. Factual accuracy -- flag anything that seems wrong
2. Consistency with brand voice: [paste guidelines]
3. Clarity -- is every point actionable and specific?
4. Missing elements -- what should be added?
5. What a senior [role] would criticize about this

Be brutally honest. List every issue, no matter how small."

YOUR INVOLVEMENT:
- Address AI-flagged issues (10-15 min)
- Final expert review (5-10 min)
- Prepare client-ready version (5 min)

OUTPUT: Polished deliverable ready for client review
```

**Step 4: Bonus Deliverables**

```
Tool: Claude / ChatGPT
Time: 20-40 minutes for all bonuses

FOR EACH BONUS:
"Based on this client project: [paste context]

Create [bonus deliverable]:
- [Specifications]
- Make it immediately actionable
- Format professionally
- Include [client's branding/context]"

YOUR INVOLVEMENT:
- Quick review per bonus (5-10 min each)
- Light customization (5 min each)

OUTPUT: 2-3 bonus deliverables that cost you almost nothing
```

**Step 5: Delivery Package**

```
Tool: Notion / Google Docs / Loom
Time: 15-20 minutes

PACKAGE INCLUDES:
1. Core deliverable(s)
2. Bonus materials
3. Walkthrough video (Loom -- 5 min recording explaining the work)
4. Next steps document
5. Feedback form

PROMPT FOR DELIVERY EMAIL:
"Write a professional delivery email for [client name].
Project: [project name]
What's included: [list deliverables]
Key highlights: [2-3 things to draw attention to]
Next steps: [what happens after they review]
Revision process: [how to request changes]

Tone: professional, confident, helpful. Not salesy."

YOUR INVOLVEMENT:
- Review email (3 min)
- Record Loom walkthrough (5 min)
- Send package (2 min)

OUTPUT: Complete delivery package sent to client
```

## Quality Assurance Checklist

Run this for EVERY client deliverable before sending:

```
ACCURACY:
[ ] All facts verified (AI hallucinates -- check claims)
[ ] Numbers and statistics sourced
[ ] Client-specific details correct (name, company, industry)
[ ] No placeholder text remaining

QUALITY:
[ ] Meets or exceeds the scope in SOW
[ ] Professional formatting
[ ] Consistent brand voice
[ ] Actionable and specific (not generic)

COMPLETENESS:
[ ] All promised deliverables included
[ ] Bonus materials attached
[ ] Next steps clear
[ ] Revision process explained

PROFESSIONALISM:
[ ] Proofread (not just spell-checked)
[ ] Client name spelled correctly everywhere
[ ] File names professional (not "draft_v3_FINAL_final.docx")
[ ] Delivery method appropriate (not a 50MB email attachment)
```

## Revision Process

**Round 1 (included in scope):**
```
1. Client submits feedback via form or email
2. AI categorizes feedback into: quick fixes / substantial changes / out of scope
3. You address quick fixes immediately (10-15 min)
4. Substantial changes get AI treatment + your review (30-60 min)
5. Out-of-scope items get the scope protection script (see 02-OFFER-ARCHITECTURE)
```

**Round 2+ (if included):**
```
Same process. If feedback contradicts Round 1 feedback, flag it:

"I notice this feedback reverses the direction from Round 1. Happy to make
the change -- I want to make sure we're aligned on the final direction so
we don't burn revision rounds going back and forth. Can we do a quick 10-min
call to lock this down?"
```

**When revisions exceed scope:**
```
"I've completed the [X] revision rounds included in our agreement. I'm happy
to continue refining -- additional revisions are $[X]/hour, billed in 30-min
increments. Want me to proceed with these changes? I estimate it'll take
[X] hours."
```

## The 80/20 of AI Delivery

What AI does well (let it):
- First drafts of anything written
- Research compilation
- Data analysis and summarization
- Template generation
- Formatting and structuring
- Brainstorming and ideation
- Email/communication drafts
- Report generation

What AI does poorly (you do this):
- Strategic judgment calls
- Understanding unstated client needs
- Navigating office politics and stakeholder dynamics
- Creative direction that requires taste
- Relationship building
- Handling sensitive conversations
- Quality judgment (knowing when "good enough" is actually good)
- Pricing and negotiation

The line is clear: AI produces, you decide.
