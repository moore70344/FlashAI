## The Six Models

Each model has a different capital requirement, time-to-revenue, and ceiling.
Pick ONE. Not two. One.

### Model 1: Service Arbitrage

**What it is:** You sell expertise. AI delivers 80% of the work. You handle client
relationships and quality control.

**Best for:**
- Skills: writing, design, marketing, data analysis, consulting
- Capital: $0-500
- Time: 15-25 hours/week
- Risk tolerance: low
- Time to first dollar: 1-3 weeks

**Revenue math:**
- 10 clients x $1,500/month retainer = $15,000/month = $180,000/year
- AI handles: research, first drafts, data processing, report generation
- You handle: client calls, strategy, final review, relationship management

**Why it works:** Clients pay for outcomes, not hours. If AI lets you deliver a
$1,500 outcome in 3 hours instead of 15, your effective rate is $500/hour.
Nobody needs to know your process.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You hate client communication. You can't
handle scope conversations. You have zero credible experience to sell.

---

### Model 2: Productized Service

**What it is:** Fixed scope. Fixed price. Fixed delivery. Every client gets the same
thing with minor customization. AI executes the playbook.

**Best for:**
- Skills: any repeatable expertise (SEO audits, financial models, brand guides)
- Capital: $200-1,000
- Time: 10-20 hours/week
- Risk tolerance: low-medium
- Time to first dollar: 2-4 weeks

**Revenue math:**
- 20 clients/month x $500/project = $10,000/month = $120,000/year
- AI handles: template generation, analysis, report creation, asset production
- You handle: intake, customization, QA, delivery

**Why it works:** Productization kills scope creep. AI makes delivery nearly
identical every time. Your margin improves with every client because your
prompts get better.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You can't define a fixed deliverable. Your
work is inherently custom. You need to do deep discovery for each client.

---

### Model 3: Info Products

**What it is:** Courses, templates, playbooks, swipe files. AI helps create and
market them. You sell one-to-many.

**Best for:**
- Skills: teaching, writing, domain expertise in a lucrative niche
- Capital: $500-2,000
- Time: 20-30 hours/week (front-loaded)
- Risk tolerance: medium
- Time to first dollar: 4-8 weeks

**Revenue math:**
- 200 sales/month x $97 course = $19,400/month = $232,800/year
- or: 50 sales/month x $497 workshop = $24,850/month
- AI handles: content creation, email marketing, social content, student support
- You handle: curriculum design, recording/writing, launch strategy

**Why it works:** Zero marginal cost per sale. AI scales your content machine.
Once built, revenue compounds.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You have no audience. You have no provable
results in your domain. You hate creating content.

---

### Model 4: Micro-SaaS

**What it is:** A small software tool solving one specific problem for one specific
audience. AI helps build it, run support, and create marketing content.

**Best for:**
- Skills: technical ability (or willingness to use no-code/AI coding tools)
- Capital: $1,000-5,000
- Time: 25-40 hours/week (front-loaded)
- Risk tolerance: medium-high
- Time to first dollar: 6-12 weeks

**Revenue math:**
- 200 users x $49/month = $9,800/month = $117,600/year
- AI handles: code generation, documentation, support responses, feature specs
- You handle: product direction, user conversations, deployment, billing

**Why it works:** Recurring revenue. Once it works, it works while you sleep.
AI dramatically reduces development time.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You can't code at all and won't learn.
You don't have a specific problem to solve. You're building a "nice to have."

---

### Model 5: Content + Monetization

**What it is:** Build an audience through content. Monetize through offers,
sponsorships, affiliates, or your own products. AI scales content production.

**Best for:**
- Skills: writing, speaking, teaching, strong opinions in a niche
- Capital: $0-500
- Time: 15-25 hours/week
- Risk tolerance: medium (longer payoff timeline)
- Time to first dollar: 4-12 weeks (affiliate/sponsorship)

**Revenue math:**
- 10,000 email subscribers x 2% conversion x $200 product = $40,000/launch
- 3 launches/year + recurring sponsorships = $120,000+/year
- AI handles: drafting content, repurposing across platforms, email sequences
- You handle: voice/brand direction, relationship building, product creation

**Why it works:** Audience is the ultimate asset. AI lets one person produce
content at the volume of a small media company.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You need money in 30 days. You have nothing
to say. You won't publish consistently.

---

### Model 6: Acquisition + Optimization

**What it is:** Buy a small, cash-flowing online business. Use AI to optimize
operations, reduce costs, and increase revenue.

**Best for:**
- Skills: operations, marketing, analysis
- Capital: $10,000-50,000+
- Time: 10-15 hours/week (after optimization)
- Risk tolerance: high
- Time to first dollar: Day 1 (business already has revenue)

**Revenue math:**
- Buy a business doing $5K/month for $50K (10x multiple)
- Optimize with AI to hit $8K/month = $96K/year
- AI handles: content updates, customer support, SEO, email marketing
- You handle: strategy, financial oversight, vendor management

**Why it works:** Skip the 0-to-1 phase entirely. AI lets you run what would
normally require 2-3 employees.

**Warning signs it's wrong for you:** You don't have the capital. You've never
run a business. You can't evaluate a P&L.
