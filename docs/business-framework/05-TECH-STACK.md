## Break-Even Analysis

```
MONTHLY TOOL COSTS:    $[X]
PRICE PER CLIENT:      $[Y]
BREAK-EVEN:            [X/Y rounded up] clients

EXAMPLE:
- Tool costs: $60/month
- Price per client: $750
- Break-even: 1 client (first client covers 12+ months of tools)
```

Your tool costs should never exceed 5% of revenue. If they do, you're
over-tooled and under-utilized.

---

## Tool Selection Rules

1. **Free tier first.** Don't pay until the free version limits you.
2. **One tool per function.** Don't run Notion AND Trello AND Asana.
3. **Monthly billing only.** Never annual until you've used it 3+ months.
4. **Cancel ruthlessly.** Audit subscriptions monthly. If you didn't use it
   last month, cancel it today.
5. **AI tools are the exception.** These are your workforce. Pay for the best
   ones you can. Saving $20/month on AI while losing $2,000/month in
   productivity is the wrong trade.
