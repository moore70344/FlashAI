# 06 - Pricing & Positioning

## Price for Value, Not for Time

The moment you quote an hourly rate, you've capped your income at
(hours available x rate). AI makes you faster, which means hourly billing
punishes efficiency. Price on outcomes instead.

---

## The Pricing Ladder

Three tiers. Every business needs three tiers. The middle tier is what most
people buy. The bottom tier exists to make the middle look reasonable. The top
tier exists for people who want the best.

### Tier 1: Entry Offer (The Foot in the Door)

```
NAME: [e.g., "The Quick Win"]
PRICE: $[250-750]

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- [Narrow-scope deliverable]
- [1-2 specific outputs]
- [Minimal revision rounds -- 1 max]

DELIVERY TIME: [3-7 days]

CLIENT TYPE: Price-sensitive buyers, first-time clients testing you,
small businesses with limited budgets

YOUR TIME: [2-4 hours total]
EFFECTIVE RATE: $[price / hours]

PURPOSE: Get them in the door. Deliver so well they upgrade.
This is not where you make money. This is where you build trust.
```

### Tier 2: Core Offer (The Money Maker)

```
NAME: [e.g., "The Full System"]
PRICE: $[1,000-3,000]

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- [Complete deliverable package]
- [3-5 specific outputs]
- [2 revision rounds]
- [1 strategy call]
- [Bonus materials]

DELIVERY TIME: [1-3 weeks]

CLIENT TYPE: Serious buyers who understand value, established
businesses with real budgets, clients who've done Tier 1 and want more

YOUR TIME: [6-12 hours total]
EFFECTIVE RATE: $[price / hours]

PURPOSE: This is your bread and butter. 60-70% of revenue comes from here.
Designed for maximum margin with AI leverage.
```

### Tier 3: Premium (The Top Shelf)

```
NAME: [e.g., "The Partnership"]
PRICE: $[3,000-10,000+]

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- [Everything in Tier 2]
- [Strategic advisory component]
- [Ongoing support for X weeks/months]
- [Priority communication]
- [Additional deliverables]
- [All bonuses]

DELIVERY TIME: [2-6 weeks or ongoing monthly]

CLIENT TYPE: Clients who value their time more than money, companies
with urgent timelines, repeat clients with high LTV potential

YOUR TIME: [15-25 hours total]
EFFECTIVE RATE: $[price / hours]

PURPOSE: High-margin work for your best clients. 20-30% of clients,
40-50% of revenue. Cherry-pick who gets this tier.
```

## Positioning Statement

### Formula

```
"I help [SPECIFIC AUDIENCE] achieve [SPECIFIC OUTCOME]
in [SPECIFIC TIMEFRAME] without [SPECIFIC PAIN THEY WANT TO AVOID]."
```

### Rules for Each Component

**SPECIFIC AUDIENCE** (not "businesses" or "entrepreneurs"):
- Bad: "small businesses"
- Good: "B2B SaaS founders doing $1-5M ARR"
- Good: "e-commerce brands spending $10K+/month on ads"
- Good: "solo consultants transitioning from corporate"

**SPECIFIC OUTCOME** (not "grow" or "improve"):
- Bad: "grow their business"
- Good: "generate 20+ qualified leads per month from LinkedIn"
- Good: "increase conversion rates by 30-50%"
- Good: "launch a productized service hitting $10K/month"

**SPECIFIC TIMEFRAME** (not "eventually"):
- Bad: "over time"
- Good: "within 60 days"
- Good: "in the first 90 days"
- Good: "before their next fundraise"

**SPECIFIC PAIN** (what they're sick of):
- Bad: "stress"
- Good: "without hiring a full marketing team"
- Good: "without spending 20 hours/week on content creation"
- Good: "without burning through their runway on agencies"

### Examples by Model

```
SERVICE ARBITRAGE:
"I help B2B SaaS founders generate 20+ qualified inbound leads per month
from LinkedIn within 60 days -- without them writing a single post."

PRODUCTIZED SERVICE:
"I help e-commerce brands identify and fix the conversion leaks costing
them $10K+/month in lost revenue -- delivered in 5 business days."

INFO PRODUCT:
"I help corporate professionals launch a profitable consulting side business
generating $5K/month within 90 days -- without quitting their day job."

MICRO-SAAS:
"I help solo attorneys complete case research in minutes instead of hours --
without expensive legal database subscriptions."

CONTENT + MONETIZATION:
"I help technical founders build a personal brand that generates deal flow --
without spending more than 3 hours per week on content."
```

## Pricing Psychology

### The Anchor Effect
Always show Tier 3 first on your pricing page. It makes Tier 2 feel reasonable.

### The Decoy Effect
Tier 1 exists to make Tier 2 the obvious choice. Make Tier 1 just limited enough
that smart buyers see Tier 2 as the better deal.

### Price Presentation
- Never say "$2,000." Say "$2,000 -- which pays for itself if [outcome math]."
- Always frame against the cost of NOT solving the problem.
- Example: "The audit is $750. Based on your traffic, the conversion issues I
  identified are costing you roughly $4,000-8,000/month. The audit pays for
  itself in the first week of implementing the fixes."

### When to Raise Prices

Raise prices when ANY of these are true:
- You're closing more than 50% of proposals (you're too cheap)
- You have a waitlist of more than 2 weeks
- You've delivered results for 5+ clients with proof
- You haven't raised prices in 3 months
- A client says "that's it?" when you quote them

Raise by 20-30% each time. Don't announce it. Just quote the new price.
Existing clients keep their current rate until renewal.

## The Anti-Discount Policy

Discounts train clients to wait for sales and signal low confidence.

**Instead of discounts, add value:**

| Client says... | Don't say... | Say instead... |
|----------------|-------------|----------------|
| "Can you do it for less?" | "Sure, I can do $X" | "The price reflects the outcome. I can adjust the scope to fit your budget -- here's what Tier 1 includes." |
| "Your competitor is cheaper" | "I'll match their price" | "They might be right for you. My clients pay for [specific differentiator]. Happy to connect you with them if price is the main factor." |
| "We don't have budget" | "I'll give you a deal" | "I understand. Let's revisit when timing is better. I'll add you to my newsletter so you get value in the meantime." |

The only acceptable "discount" is a case study discount for your first 3-5 clients:
"I'm offering a 30% reduced rate for my next 3 clients in exchange for a detailed
case study and testimonial I can use in my marketing. Interested?"

This is an exchange of value, not a discount.
